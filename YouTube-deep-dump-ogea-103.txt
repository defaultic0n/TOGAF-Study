Question 2: Which phase of the ADM has the purpose to develop an Enterprise Architecture Capability?
A: Phase A
B: Phase G
C: Phase B
D: Preliminary Phase
Answer: D
Explanation: The Preliminary Phase establishes the foundational elements, governance and framework needed for an EA capability. Phases A, B and G assume that capability already exists or focus on other tasks.

Question 3: Which statement about Requirements Management is most correct?

C: The purpose of Requirements Management is to process change requests.
D: Stakeholder requirements are captured once in Phase A and managed throughout the ADM cycle.
Answer: A
Explanation: Requirements Management is a continuous process at the centre of architecture development, not a step within each phase. Processing change requests is only one part of it, and requirements are revisited throughout the cycle.

Question 5: Which of the following is a responsibility of an Architecture Board?

Question 6: What is used to structure architectural information in an orderly way so that it can be processed to meet stakeholder needs?

Develop just enough architecture to identify options, identify candidate building blocks for each project, assess solution providers and their viability, and document a draft Implementation and Migration Plan.
Answer: B
Explanation: The end-to-end Target Architecture already gives the direction, so the work can be organized by identifying, prioritizing and sizing projects from it, with options, risks and trade-offs documented for stakeholders. Option A ignores the completed target, and options C and D look outward instead of using internal work.
